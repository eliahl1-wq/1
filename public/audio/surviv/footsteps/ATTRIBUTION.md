# Surviv footstep audio attribution

The recordings in this directory are adapted from **Footsteps on different
surfaces**, assembled by congusbongus and published under CC BY 3.0:

- https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces
- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The source pack credits the following recordings:

- Grass and dirt: `footstep-grass.wav` by swuing (CC BY 3.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38874/
- Gravel: `Gravel Footsteps` by Ali_6868 (CC0)
  https://freesound.org/people/Ali_6868/packs/21608/
- Asphalt/concrete: `footstep-concrete.wav` by swuing (CC BY 3.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38873/
- Indoor/tile: `footstep-concrete.wav` by swuing and `Squeaky footstep.wav`
  by ceberation (CC BY 3.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/ceberation/sounds/235524/
- Wood: `footstep-wood.wav` by swuing (CC BY 3.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38876/
- Water: `Water Footsteps` by EminYILDIRIM and `footstep-concrete.wav` by
  swuing (CC BY 3.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/EminYILDIRIM/sounds/608663/
- Metal: `fboots on aluminum ladder 01` by Eelke (CC BY 3.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/Eelke/sounds/462598/

Changes made for this game include selecting individual takes, renaming and
organizing them by material, trimming playback in-engine, level balancing,
filtering, and subtle pitch/stereo variation.
