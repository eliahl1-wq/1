# Surviv door audio

The door cues in this directory are mastered derivatives of selected files from
**Various Sound Effects** by `laleksic`, published on OpenGameArt.org under
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):

- Source: https://opengameart.org/node/143670
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original files used: `door_open.wav`, `door_creak_open.wav`,
  `door_close.wav`, `door_creak_close.wav`, `manhole_open.wav`,
  `grind_metal.wav`, `clang_metal.wav`, and `lock.wav`

The recordings are trimmed and level-matched by
`scripts/generate-surviv-door-sounds.py`. No Surviv.io audio asset is included.
